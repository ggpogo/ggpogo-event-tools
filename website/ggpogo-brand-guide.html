Brand Overview

Garden Grove PoGo is a thriving community hub for local Pokémon GO trainers. Founded in January 2024, we've grown to over 6,600 members who gather to coordinate raids, celebrate special events, and build friendships around a shared passion for the game.

Our brand identity reflects our core values: welcoming, inclusive, enthusiastic, and genuine. We're building a space where trainers of all levels feel at home—from casual players to competitive raiders.

Brand Personality

We are approachable but knowledgeable, playful but respectful, energetic but authentic. Our design and voice prioritize clarity, warmth, and a sense of community over corporate polish or excessive formality.

Typography

Our type system uses two carefully selected Google Fonts that work together to create a warm, accessible, and distinctive visual voice.

Typeface Selection

Fredoka

Used for all headings and display text. Fredoka is friendly, modern, and distinctive—it carries the personality of the brand.

Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

Nunito

Used for body text and supporting copy. Nunito is warm, legible, and pairs perfectly with Fredoka.

Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700

Logo System

The Garden Grove PoGo logo exists in two primary forms: the full wordmark and the mark-only icon. Use the wordmark for official headers, letterheads, and primary branding. Use the mark for favicons, profile pictures, and small-space contexts.

Logo Variations

Mark Only

Use for favicons, app icons, profile pictures, and social media avatars. Maintains recognition at small sizes.

Full Wordmark

Use for headers, letterheads, official documents, and anywhere the full brand identity is needed.

Clear Space & Sizing

Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark. This preserves visual breathing room and prevents the logo from feeling cramped.

Minimum Size: 40px × 40px for the mark, 150px width for the wordmark. Never scale below these minimums as legibility will suffer.

Logo Don'ts

Never:
  • Stretch or distort the logo
  • Change the colors
  • Add shadows, gradients, or effects
  • Rotate the logo
  • Place on busy or clashing backgrounds
  • Abbreviate or modify the wordmark

Design System

Our design philosophy centers on papercraft simplicity—warm, approachable surfaces with generous whitespace, soft rounded corners, and gentle depth. Think handmade cards and printed materials; not cold corporate design.

Card & Surface Style

All surfaces use the paper base color (#FFFCF2) with subtle borders in paper-edge (#E8DEC0). Rounded corners are 12–16px. Shadows are soft and minimal (0 2px 8px rgba(13, 45, 82, 0.06)).

Card Example

This is a properly styled card. Notice the warm paper background, soft border, rounded corners, and gentle shadow. Use cards for event listings, announcements, testimonials, and grouped content.

Interactive Elements

Buttons use pill-shaped styling (border-radius: 50px) and always include a hover state with subtle lift (transform: translateY(-2px)).

Spacing & Grid

Use a consistent spacing scale to maintain visual rhythm: 0.5rem (8px), 1rem (16px), 1.5rem (24px), 2rem (32px), 3rem (48px). These units scale across all elements—margins, padding, gaps—to create a cohesive system.

Layout: Use flexible grid systems that adapt to content. For multi-column layouts, aim for consistent column widths with equal gaps (1–2rem). Always prioritize readability and mobile responsiveness.

Patterns & Imagery

Wave Pattern

The wave background asset is our signature pattern. It's organic, flowing, and suggests growth—perfect for hero sections and full-width backgrounds. Use the wave to frame important content or create visual interest in large open spaces.

Photography & Illustration Style

Photography: Favor warm, natural lighting and authentic community moments over staged corporate photography. Real meetup photos, candid trainer shots, and outdoor game-play moments resonate with our audience.

Illustrations: When illustrations are needed, use a simple, friendly style that complements Fredoka. Avoid overly realistic or overly flat styles—aim for a mid-ground that feels hand-crafted and approachable.

Color in Imagery: Support the brand palette by incorporating blue, green, and orange accents in photography and illustrations. Warm, natural lighting reinforces the "outdoor community" feeling.

Icons

Icons should be simple, outline-style, and easily recognizable at small sizes. Use a consistent stroke weight and corner radius. Icons inherit color from their parent context (links use blue, success states use green, etc.).

Tone of Voice

Our voice is enthusiastic but genuine, welcoming but never condescending, knowledgeable but accessible. We're speaking to trainers who share a passion for Pokémon GO—acknowledge that shared passion and make them feel at home.

Voice Characteristics

  • Friendly: Use conversational language and warm punctuation (exclamation points are fine in the right context)
  • Clear: Avoid jargon; explain the "why" not just the "what"
  • Inclusive: Welcome trainers of all levels, backgrounds, and experience
  • Action-oriented: Use active voice; tell people what they can do, not what they can't

Tone Examples

✓ Correct: "Join us Wednesday at 6 PM for Raid Hour! We're tackling a 5-star raid and would love to have you. Bring your best team and meet some new trainers!"
✓ Correct: "New to the group? That's awesome! Head to our Discord to introduce yourself and find your raid squad. We're all here to help."
✗ Avoid: "RAID HOUR IS HAPPENING LET'S GOOOO 🔥🔥🔥" (too chaotic)
✗ Avoid: "It is with great pleasure that we announce a raid event." (too formal for a community)
✗ Avoid: "Only trainers with max-level teams need apply." (exclusionary)

Do's and Don'ts

Design

✓ Do

Use blue and green together for visual cohesion

✗ Don't

Introduce red, pink, or purple—they're not on brand

✓ Do

Use plenty of whitespace and padding

✗ Don't

Crowd content or use busy dark backgrounds

✓ Do

Round corners at 12–16px for a friendly feel

✗ Don't

Use sharp 90-degree corners or harsh effects

✓ Do

Use Fredoka + Nunito consistently

✗ Don't

Mix in other typefaces—stick to the system

Content & Community

✓ Do

Link to official Niantic sources and well-established community tools

✗ Don't

Promote dataminers, unofficial maps, or paid raid services

✓ Do

Celebrate community wins and inclusive moments

✗ Don't

Post divisive content or gatekeep the community

✓ Do

Use warm, authentic community photos

✗ Don't

Use overly staged or corporate-looking images

✓ Do

Welcome trainers of all levels

✗ Don't

Use em dashes (–) or en dashes (—) in web copy
