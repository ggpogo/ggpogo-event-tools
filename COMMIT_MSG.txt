Fix the name field closing after one letter (v2.21.1)

On the Code Drop, Giveaway and Gacha claim screens the first keystroke
counted as a known name, which swapped the field for "Playing as",
closed the keyboard and saved a one-letter name. "Playing as" now only
shows for a name the screen opened with or loaded from the profile.
Saved names under two characters are treated as unset so affected
people get the field back, pre-filled, to correct it.
